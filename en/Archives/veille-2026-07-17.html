AI enters the factory; partners head to court

The insurer disconnects the drivers and plugs the agents – Apple drags OpenAI before the judge while Microsoft coach its salespeople – inflation falls below 2% but the ECB keeps its finger on the trigger – and Mistral courtes 3 billion so that it does not end up vassal. Friday morning roundup. Here we go.

In 30 seconds

  • Insurance: AI is moving into production — Allianz and Anthropic are co-developing custom agents, Allianz Partners is already processing 90% of its claims automatically (compared to 10%), and the year 2026 is that of industrialization, not pilots.
  • Tech & AI: Alliances turn to vinegar — Apple is pursuing OpenAI for theft of secrets, Microsoft is training its salespeople to denigrate OpenAI and Anthropic — while the unicorn machine is still running (Emergent, Together AI, ~90 unicorns in 2026).
  • Economy: French inflation falls to 1.7% and the euro area falls below 2% for the first time since the end of 2021 — but the ECB refuses to let go, with a deposit rate of 2.25%, growth set at 0.9%.
  • Regulatory: ACPR, ANSSI and the Banque de France sign a cyber cooperation agreement, 2026 becomes DORA's "year of supervision", and ransomware now captures 76% of the cyber claims of the semester.
1Insurance & Insurtech

Insurer disconnects pilots, plugs agents

The demo is finished, place at the assembly line. Allianz has just sealed an agreement with a giant of the sector — Anthropic — for embarking its models in insurance operations : tailor-made agents orchestrating workflows from end to end, from collecting coins to compensation, recording each decision to remain traceable and compliant. The numbers give rise to vertigo: Allianz Partners has increased from 10% to 90% automatic claims processing, the detection of fraud from Allianz France wins +50 per cent detection rate and -40 per cent investigation time, and at AXA 85% of simple folders are settled Without a human hand. The sector is officially moving into theera of industrialization, between pioneers and backwards.

When the agent manages 9 out of 10 claims, the real cost item is no longer labour but governance — who audits, who responds, who assumes when the machine is wrong.

2Tech & AI

Apple drags OpenAI to court, Microsoft coachs its salespeople to dislike

The co-operation varnish has melted all of a sudden. On July 10, Apple assigned OpenAI to alleged theft of industrial secrets around its future AI devices, accusing OpenAI of having engineer debauched and bought io Products ($6.4 billion) to accelerate — a complaint which could serious restraint of material ambitions Camp Altman. Meanwhile, Microsoft does not embarrass itself with diplomacy: its salesmen are now trained to denigrate OpenAI and Anthropic Facing Copilot. All on the background of flight forward: Emerging becomes Unicorn at $1.5 billion in one year, Together AI ralles $800 million to jump to $8.3 billion, and 2026 has already hit nearly 90 new unicorns. The lesson for the leader: AI is no longer a lab, it is a strategic asset — and at this level of stake, yesterday's partners become the litigators of tomorrow. Check that your supplier contracts survive the jam.

3Economy & Finance

Inflation falls below 2 %, ECB keeps its finger tight

The number is there, the trigger is not. French inflation fell to 1.7% and the euro area to 1.8%, the symbolic threshold of 2% for the first time since the end of 2021. What do you want to calm down the hawks? Not really: Governor Kocher reaffirms the target and refuses to release the pressure, when the transcripts still showed a fear of persistent inflation. As a result, key rates remain high — 2.40%, 2.25% deposit, 2.65% marginal loan — and the arbitrage rate-high-counter-inflation continues to slowing down an already soft activity, growth set at around 0.9%.

Inflation that ranks does not mechanically reopen the credit valve. As long as the ECB plays credibility against the economic situation, the cost of money remains your most rigid variable — budget expensive, refinance early.

4Regulation & Risk

ACPR, ANSSI, Bank of France: Trio closes ranks

The regulator stops asking politely. On 3 July, ANSSI, ACPR and the Bank of France signed a cooperation agreement to enhance the exchange of information under NIS2 and DORA, three authorities join forces the threat. The message is clear: 2026 is the"Monitoring year" where ACPR no longer asks where you are but requires evidencewith a DORA documentation is becoming more precise on incidents, ICT risk and provider contracts. And the urgency is real: ransomware now concentrates 76% of cyber claims in the first half of the year (compared to 46% a year earlier), on a cyber insurance market that exceeds €1.1 billion, when an attack costs on average €466,000 to a French SME. The message for the manager: Your incident log is now as good as a balance sheet — the supervisor wants to see the tests, not the right intentions.

5People & Work

AI moves to the executive office, training stays in the locker room

The tool is everywhere, the instructions for use nowhere. According to APEC, half of executives now use AI at least once a week, +15 points in one year 62% in younger age groups, and 66% believe that AI control will be a key competence for their future. The Hic: only 29% have been formed, a gap between actual use and competence that exposes as much as it opens — and AI is already redefining in depth the managerial professions. On the market side, the balance of power turns: recruitment difficulties fall sharply (−15 pts since 2022), while the companies lift foot and AI is already rewriting HR processes.

Distributing AI licenses without training is arming a team without a shooting range. The real spending of the summer is not the tool — it is the time to rise to competence.

6Sovereignty & Infra

Mistral courted 3 billion to not finish vassal

Sovereignty has a price, and it counts in billions. Mistral accelerates on corporate AI and European sovereignty, with a raise of approximately €3 billion that would target almost €20 billion of valuation and a global partnership with Naver Cloud sealed on July 8 — France hammering it out "to refuse technological vassalization". But the infrastructure remains captive: 99% of global traffic is via submarine cables with 90% of Europe-USA bandwidth controlled by GAFAM, pushing the Commission to release €347 million to secure sensitive areas. On the data center side, Europe remains fragmented (~300 in France versus 487 in Germany), and it's very much recalled that "The global sovereignty of data centers cannot be achieved today".

A sovereign model on foreign pipes and servers remains a trompe l'oeil independence. The question is not "what model," but "who holds the catch below."
