AI takes action; Europe looks for the off switch

The AI agentique leaves the laboratory to pilot disaster management alone – the unicorn frenzy is in full swing while 88% of AI's money remains American – the ECB is about to lift its foot on 24 July when inflation falls into line – and the "kill switch" Anthropic reminds Europe that holds the handle. Thursday morning roundup. Here we go.

In 30 seconds

  • Insurance: the AI agent moves from experimentation to production — Maif awarded for its claims management, Cleva decorated "Luminary" by Celent — but the "silent AI" closes: since January, explicit AI exclusions (ISO/Verisk) are required in contracts.
  • Tech & AI: Unicorn frenzy does not weaken — Emergent becomes unicorn ($1.5 billion) in a year, Together AI raises $800 million — but 88% of global AI funding remains captured by the United States alone.
  • Economy: French inflation fell to 1.7% in June; the ECB is expected to maintain its rates on 24 July, with the dreaded increase moving away as energy stabilizes, with 2026 growth set at 0.7%.
  • Regulatory: cyber industrialise — +27% of cyber attacks claimed in France (1,347), average cost €180,000 — and the PCRA is exercising its DORA supervision (incidents, ICT risk, contract providers).
1Insurance & Insurtech

Agent AI leaves PowerPoint for the disaster room

No more riders running in loops. Agent AI — these systems that reason, plan, and act alone from end to end — settles where the density of decision-making is highest: claims management and specialized subscription. The trophies follow: Maif has just been Award-winning for its data-driven disaster management, and Cleva won the distinction "Luminary" by Celent, the highest, for a platform where AI is no longer a diaper but the conductor. But the festival has a regulatory setback: the "silent AI" — a risk neither covered nor excluded — is closing, as since January the explicit AI exclusions (ISO/Verisk) are included in contractsand the governance challenges of the agent become the real subject of comex. On the financing side, the wave does not fall: Panora has lifted €3.3 million to help brokers and Continuity €10 million for proactive identification of business risks.

The question is no longer "do you need an agent?" but "who answers when the agent is wrong?"

2Tech & AI

The unicorn machine runs full, but she speaks American

The meter's freaking out. The Indian startup Emergent has become unicorn in Just one year, valued $1.5 billion after a $130 million Series C, when the neocloud Together AI ralled $800 million to jump to $8.3 billion — all in a year that has already struck nearly 90 new unicorns. We even saw an AI agent. close a $100M raise alone against 130 investors. But behind the euphoria, a very unbalanced map: 88% of global AI financing is captured by the United States alone, leaving Europe crumbs and pride. France bets on its champion: Mistral pushes its open-weight model and an RRA increased from $20 million to $400 million in one year. The leader's lesson: capital tap flows, but it's connected across the Atlantic — knowing where the money that finances your suppliers comes from is no longer a geopolitical detail.

3Economy & Finance

The ECB sets the trigger, inflation ranks

The worst-case scenario is moving away. Inflation in France fell back to 1.7% in June, below 2% as the euro area, after the spring energy surge — enough to disarm the hawks. The ECB, which did not exclude a screw trick, now lets it filter out. maintain its rates on 24 July if the energy remains stable ; the July increase seems less and less likely. In the background, INSEE holds its note " Reinvigorated inflation, weakened growth » and holds the growth 2026 to 0.7%, affected by the spring oil shock, while CAC 40 is sailing around 8 300–8 400 points.

The meeting of the 24th will be less a decision than a credibility test — a confirmed pause, and the refinancing window remains open; a slip of the barrel, and the finger returns to the trigger.

4Regulation & Risk

Cyber goes on an industrial scale, DORA pulls out the logbook

The threat no longer alters, it industrializes. COMCCYBER-MI identified 1,347 cyberattacks claimed against France in 2025, or +27%, dominated by the DDoS hacktivists; Verspieren's Cyber White Paper 2026 makes it a major business and insurance risk, at 180 000 € average cost, while the attack enter the era of industrialization. In the face of this, the supervisor tightened: the ACPR made DORA, AI, value-for-money and LCB-FT its priorities 2026, with three very concrete targets on the side Digital operational resilience : incident management, ICT risk framework and compliance of provider contracts. Timeline reminder: the "high risk" rules of the AI Act for finance will apply as of December 2, 2027 — but the Cyber Resilience Act is already biting, with sanctions of up to 4% of global turnover. The message: Your PRA is now worth as much as your balance sheet, and the regulator wants to see the tests, not the intentions.

5People & Work

The big swing: this time, AI targets the white collar

The wave no longer hits the workshop but the office. 99% of leaders now anticipate at least a few AI-related job deletions within two years, and the cursor moved upwards: it is the major change in management jobs, with an exposure that climbs to ~29% for engineering and ~27% for legal and finance. In France, the CFE-CGC agitates the figure of 5 million skilled jobs exposed, while junior posts — research, synthesis — decrease as AI absorbs repetitive task. Salutary Nuance: the indicator measures an exposure of tasks, not their destruction — and companies quickly discover that AI, supposed to offer "time and meaning", can also increase the load. The real HR question of the summer: if we don't recruit juniors anymore, Where will seniors be trained? whose judgment becomes, precisely, the rare commodity?

6Sovereignty & Infra

The "kill switch" Anthropic: Europe discovers who holds the handle

Sovereignty ceased to be a theme of colloquium on the day the switch jumped. On June 12, the United States activated what the specialists call the "kill switch": by an export control directive, Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models were disconnected without notice for all non-American users, brutally materialising a risk of dependency that Europe feared without believing. The Ifri sees a new form of dependency — no longer at the servers, but at the Access permissions — while the episode triggers a global rush to sovereign AI. The great winner is named Mistral: Arthur Mensch drew his open source by hammering "we exist outside the control of a state"When the spectrum of the American Kill Switch potentially extends from the model to the cloud, then to the cyber tool. The lesson for the leader: a service that you can't turn on yourself is not an asset, it's a revocable option — and the exit plan AI just passed to the top of the stack.
